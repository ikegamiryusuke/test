What we offer

Media and creative services

Japan Between Stops creates original digital media and provides Japan-focused creative and research services online. Commercial offerings may include the following.

Japan market research

Japan-focused market research, competitor research, cultural context, and practical research support for overseas companies, creators, and projects.

Localization & social content

English-to-Japanese and Japanese-to-English localization, social media content planning, and market-aware adaptation for audiences in Japan and abroad.

Short-form content

Scripts, captions, creative concepts, and digital deliverables for short-form video and social media, supplied online as documents, PDFs, or other agreed digital formats.

Digital publications

Original field notes, guides, essays, and other digital editorial products about everyday life, movement, and travel in Japan.

Photo & video licensing

Licensing of original photographs and video footage captured in Japan for editorial, commercial, or creative use under agreed license terms.

Creative production

Commissioned vertical video, Japan-based footage production, and creator content for brands and organizations when the project fits the Japan Between Stops editorial world.

Customer information

Policies

Refunds & disputes

For digital products, refunds are available for duplicate charges or material access/technical issues reported within 7 days of purchase. For commissioned services, any refund is based on the portion of work not yet performed. If a charge appears incorrect, please contact us first so we can review it promptly. Statutory consumer rights remain unaffected.

Cancellations

Digital products cannot normally be cancelled after access or delivery has been provided. Commissioned services may be cancelled before work begins for a full refund. After work begins, completed work is non-refundable and any unused prepaid portion may be refunded.

Delivery

Digital products are delivered electronically. Licensing files and commissioned deliverables are supplied digitally according to the scope and schedule agreed with the customer before purchase.

Privacy

This website does not request payment-card details directly. When payments are offered, payment information is handled by the applicable payment provider, including Stripe. Personal information received for support or project delivery is used only to provide the requested service and meet legal or accounting obligations.

Licensing

Photo and video licensing is subject to the usage rights stated in the applicable quote, invoice, or license agreement. Copyright is not transferred unless explicitly agreed in writing.
